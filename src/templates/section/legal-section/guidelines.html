Community Guidelines

A respectful and safe space for everyone.

🙌
1. Respect Others

Always engage respectfully. Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks are not tolerated. Debate ideas, not people.

🎯
2. Stay On Topic

Keep discussions focused on relevant subjects. Avoid spamming, trolling, or derailing conversations.

💡
3. Contribute Value

Share knowledge that benefits the community. Avoid misleading information, plagiarism, or low-effort content.

🔒
4. Protect Privacy

Do not share sensitive or personal information — yours or others'. Respect confidentiality at all times.

⚖️
5. Follow the Law

Illegal content, activities, or encouragement of harmful behavior is strictly prohibited.

🚨
6. Report Issues

If you see harmful or rule-breaking behavior, use the Report Issue feature. Together, we keep the community safe.
